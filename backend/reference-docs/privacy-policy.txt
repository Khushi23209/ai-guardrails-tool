TechNova Privacy Policy
Effective Date: February 1, 2026

1. TechNova collects the customer's name, email address, billing address, and shipping address.
2. Phone numbers are collected only when customers provide them during checkout or support requests.
3. TechNova collects order numbers and purchase history to provide order-related services.
4. Payment card numbers are processed by certified payment providers and are not stored on TechNova's servers.
5. Website usage data includes browser type, device type, IP address, and pages visited.
6. TechNova uses cookies to maintain login sessions and remember shopping-cart contents.
7. Analytics cookies may be disabled through the website's cookie settings.
8. Customer account data is stored using encrypted databases.
9. Passwords are stored using one-way cryptographic hashing.
10. TechNova retains completed order records for 7 years for accounting and legal requirements.
11. Customer support messages are retained for 3 years after the support case is closed.
12. Customers may request deletion of their account data through the privacy request form.
13. Deletion requests are normally completed within 30 calendar days.
14. Certain records may be retained when required by law or for fraud prevention.
15. TechNova does not sell customer personal information to advertising companies.
16. Customer information may be shared with payment processors to complete transactions.
17. Shipping information may be shared with delivery companies to fulfill orders.
18. TechNova may share information with government authorities when legally required.
19. Employees can access customer information only when it is necessary for their job responsibilities.
20. Privacy questions can be submitted to privacy@technova.example.
