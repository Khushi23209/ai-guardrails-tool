TechNova Refund Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of the purchase date.
2. Electronics have a shorter return window of 15 calendar days.
3. Refund requests must include the original order number.
4. Products must be returned in unused condition unless the product is defective.
5. Original packaging must be included for electronics and computer accessories.
6. Refunds are not available for products damaged through customer misuse.
7. Digital products are eligible for a refund within 7 calendar days of purchase.
8. Digital products are not refundable after they have been downloaded more than twice.
9. Shipping charges are refundable only when TechNova ships an incorrect or defective item.
10. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs for change-of-mind returns.
11. TechNova provides a prepaid return label for defective products.
12. Refunds are processed within 7 business days after TechNova receives the returned item.
13. Bank processing may require an additional 3 to 5 business days before the refund appears.
14. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
15. Cash refunds are not provided for purchases originally made by credit or debit card.
16. Items purchased during clearance sales are eligible for exchange but are not eligible for refunds.
17. Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
18. Promotional discounts are deducted from the refunded amount.
19. A customer may request a replacement instead of a refund for a defective product.
20. Refund requests must be submitted through the TechNova customer support portal.
