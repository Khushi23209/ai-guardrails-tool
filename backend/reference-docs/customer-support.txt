TechNova Customer Support Policy
Effective Date: April 1, 2026

1. Customer support is available Monday through Saturday.
2. Support hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST.
3. Customer support is closed on Sundays.
4. Support is available through email, live chat, and the customer support portal.
5. The official customer support email is support@technova.example.
6. Live chat is available during regular support hours.
7. Email support normally responds within 24 hours.
8. Support portal requests normally receive a response within 1 business day.
9. Live-chat requests are normally answered within 5 minutes.
10. Customers should provide their order number when contacting support about an order.
11. Warranty cases require the product serial number.
12. Refund requests must be submitted through the refund request form.
13. Support agents can provide order status and tracking information.
14. Support agents cannot change the original payment method used for an order.
15. Billing disputes are forwarded to the billing team for investigation.
16. Billing investigations normally take up to 3 business days.
17. Unresolved support cases can be escalated to a senior support specialist.
18. Customers may request escalation after 2 unsuccessful support responses.
19. Senior support specialists normally respond to escalated cases within 2 business days.
20. Customers can check the status of an open support case using their case number.
