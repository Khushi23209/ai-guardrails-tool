TechNova Shipping Policy
Effective Date: March 1, 2026

1. Standard domestic shipping costs $5.99 for orders below $50.
2. Standard domestic shipping is free for orders of $50 or more.
3. Standard domestic delivery normally takes 4 to 7 business days.
4. Express shipping costs $14.99 per order.
5. Express shipping normally takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch.
6. Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on business days are normally processed the same day.
7. Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST are processed on the following business day.
8. TechNova does not process shipments on Sundays or public holidays.
9. Customers receive a tracking number after their order has been dispatched.
10. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become active.
11. International shipping is available to 18 countries.
12. International shipping starts at $24.99 per package.
13. International delivery normally takes 7 to 15 business days.
14. Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the customer.
15. TechNova does not guarantee delivery dates for international shipments.
16. Orders containing multiple products may be shipped in separate packages.
17. Customers must provide a complete and accurate delivery address.
18. TechNova is not responsible for delays caused by incorrect addresses supplied by customers.
19. Packages returned because of an incorrect address may require a new shipping fee.
20. Customers should contact support within 48 hours of receiving a damaged package.
