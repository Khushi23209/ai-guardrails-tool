TechNova Product Warranty Policy
Effective Date: January 15, 2026

1. TechNova provides a 12-month limited warranty on eligible electronic products.
2. The warranty begins on the original purchase date shown on the sales receipt.
3. Warranty coverage applies only to products purchased through TechNova or an authorized reseller.
4. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
5. Warranty coverage includes defective internal electronic components.
6. The warranty does not cover accidental drops or physical impact damage.
7. Water or liquid damage is excluded from warranty coverage.
8. Damage caused by unauthorized repairs is not covered by the warranty.
9. Normal cosmetic wear, including scratches and discoloration, is not covered.
10. Batteries are covered for 6 months from the purchase date.
11. Warranty claims require proof of purchase.
12. Customers must provide the product serial number when submitting a claim.
13. Warranty claims can be submitted through the TechNova warranty portal.
14. TechNova reviews standard warranty claims within 5 business days.
15. Approved warranty repairs are normally completed within 10 business days after inspection.
16. If a product cannot be repaired, TechNova may provide a replacement of the same model.
17. Replacement products receive the remainder of the original warranty period.
18. Warranty coverage is not transferable to a second owner.
19. Customers are responsible for securely packaging products sent for warranty inspection.
20. TechNova does not reimburse unauthorized repair expenses.
